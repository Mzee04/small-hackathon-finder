Read this before picking one – honest limits Honest limits: this list only covers hackathons listed on Devpost. Registration counts are not submissions - most registered teams never submit, so real odds are usually better than the numbers suggest, but the ratio is only a heuristic, not a prediction. Prize amounts are self-reported by organisers and are not verified, may be split across many tracks or teams, and currencies are shown as listed without conversion, with a rough US-dollar value next to non-dollar prizes. For ranking, prizes are converted to US dollars using approximate, hard-coded exchange rates (taken 2026-10-26, not live), and prizes in a currency without a known rate are listed after all the others. Always read each hackathon's rules page for eligibility, team-size limits and exact prize conditions before entering.
